# HB Supplier Order

Subtitle: Supplier ordering insights

HB Supplier Order helps authorised Hot Bargain employees review supplier-ordering information while browsing supported supplier product pages in Safari.

Use the Safari extension to:

- view internal purchase records for supplier items;
- review sales history and average sale prices for an authorised store;
- identify supplier top sellers and ordering priorities;
- open the full ordering assistant from Safari or the Hot Bargain ordering portal.

An existing authorised Hot Bargain account is required. The app does not provide public registration, place supplier orders automatically, or make purchasing decisions for the user.

The Safari extension is designed for iPhone and iPad and is available only to authorised Hot Bargain employees and purchasing personnel in Australia.

Keywords: supplier,ordering,purchase,sales,inventory,business

## What's New in Version 1.5.0

- Records supplier categories while you browse supported supplier category pages, so Hot Bargain products can be organised by each supplier's own categories. It can be switched off for each supplier in the assistant.
- Adds "Capture all categories" to the assistant: it visits the open supplier website's category pages one at a time at a limited rate, shows progress, and can be stopped at any time.
- If iOS suspends the supplier tab, return to it and tap Continue to resume from where the capture stopped, or Retry failed for categories that could not be read.
